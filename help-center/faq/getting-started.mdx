## Overview

Sign up, upload your docs, and start scanning for accuracy!

## Quick Steps

1. Go to `https://twudocdetective.com/signup`
2. Create your account
3. Click "New Project" and upload or connect your docs
4. Press "Scan Now" to begin analysis

## See Results

Your Document Dashboard shows flagged issues and suggestions to update your technical content.
