---
title: Can't Log In to Doc Detective
description: Fix common login issues with TWU Doc Detective.
---

## Symptoms

- Login page reloads
- "Invalid email or password" error

## Solutions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reset your password">
    ````
    Click "Forgot password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your email.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check browser settings">
    ````
    Ensure cookies are enabled and disable browser plugins that block scripts.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
