---
title: Run Your First Document Scan
description: Quick guide to scan documentation using TWU Doc Detective.
---

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a new project">
    ````
    Click "New Project" after logging in. Give your project a name.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add documentation sources">
    ````
    Upload files or connect a GitHub repo. Supported: Markdown, HTML.
    ````
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start scan">
    ````
    Press the "Scan Now" button. Wait for analysis to complete. Results will display issues found across your docs.
    ````
  </Step>
</Steps>
